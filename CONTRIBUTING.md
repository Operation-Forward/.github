# Contributing

Thank you for helping Operation Forward.

- **Questions and ideas:** open an issue on the repository it concerns, or email
  contact@operationforward.org.
- **Security problems:** never in a public issue; follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Changes:** open a pull request against `main`. Every change goes through a pull request, the CI
  gates (tests, Semgrep, Trivy, workflow lint) must pass, and a maintainer reviews and merges it.
- **Brand assets:** use the files in [opfor-design](https://github.com/Operation-Forward/opfor-design)
  as they are; its brand guide explains clear space, colours and what not to change.
