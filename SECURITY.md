# Security policy

Operation Forward runs services for veterans and volunteers, and we take reports about their
security seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue. Instead:

- on a public repository, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on GitHub, or
- email **contact@operationforward.org** with "Security" in the subject.

Include what you found, where (URL or repository and file), steps to reproduce, and the impact you
expect. We will confirm we received it, keep you informed while we fix it, and credit you when the fix
ships if you would like.

## Scope

Our repositories under [github.com/Operation-Forward](https://github.com/Operation-Forward) and the
services they run, including the Operation Forward Learning platform.

Please test only against accounts you own, do not access or change other people's data, and do not
degrade the service for others (no denial-of-service or spam testing).
